# Connect Google Workspace

Connect a Google account to let the agent use the Google services you authorize
for a Mapache workspace: Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Calendar.

If you already added your account but the agent cannot see it, confirm that the
account is enabled for the selected workspace, then **restart the workspace**.
Google connections load when its session starts. Refreshing the browser or
starting a new chat does not apply a connection to an already-running session.

## Add and Enable an Account

1. Select the Mapache workspace where you want the agent to use Google services.
2. Open **Manage Google Workspace** in the top navigation. On smaller screens,
   look in the **More** menu.
3. Choose **Add Google account**, select the services you need, and choose
   **Read only** or **Read and write** access. Read-only access is the default.
4. Select **Start Google authorization** and complete Google's consent flow in
   the popup.
5. Return to the account list. Use **Refresh Google Workspace** if the new account
   has not appeared yet.
6. Confirm that the account says **Authorized** and has a checkmark. The checkmark
   means it is enabled for the selected workspace. If it shows an unplug icon,
   click that toggle to enable it.

Saved accounts can be reused across Mapache workspaces, but each workspace has
its own account selection. An account appearing in the list does not by itself
mean it is enabled for the workspace you are using.

## Restart and Verify

Finish any active agent task before restarting.

1. Close the Google Workspace dialog.
2. Click **Pause workspace** beside the workspace selector.
3. Wait until the workspace has stopped, then click **Start workspace** (the Play
   button).
4. Wait for the Agent view to be ready, then ask the agent to use an authorized
   Google service. For example, if you enabled Drive:

   > Use the Google Workspace connection to list five recent files in my Google Drive.

If the workspace was already off when you connected Google, just start it.
Mapache loads the connection automatically as the `google-workspace` MCP server;
you do not need to add a separate entry in **Manage MCP servers**.

## If the Agent Still Cannot Access Google

- **No Google tools are available:** Confirm you selected the correct Mapache
  workspace, the account is enabled there, and you paused and started the
  workspace after connecting or changing it.
- **The account says Reconnect required:** Edit the account and complete Google
  authorization again, then restart the workspace.
- **A service or write operation is unavailable:** Edit the account, select the
  required service and access level, complete authorization again, and restart.
  Only the services and permissions you authorize are available to the agent.
- **Tools appear but requests fail:** Keep the exact error from the agent. Google
  account permissions or your organization's administrator restrictions may
  prevent access. If the account remains authorized and enabled after a restart,
  report the workspace name, affected service, and error for troubleshooting.

Repeat the restart after changing the connected account, authorized services, or
access level. Disabling the account for a workspace also requires restarting its
active session to remove the loaded Google tools.
